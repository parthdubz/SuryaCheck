SuryaCheck

SuryaCheck watches a solar plant's inverters and compares how much power they actually produced against how much they were expected to produce, given the weather that day. When an inverter falls behind for several days in a row, it gets flagged as a likely fault, with an estimate of the energy and rupees lost.
