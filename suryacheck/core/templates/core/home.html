SuryaCheck

Know which solar inverter is losing you money - before you climb the roof.

SuryaCheck watches every inverter in a plant and compares what it actually produced against what it should have produced, given that day's sunlight and heat. When one falls behind for several days in a row, you get a ranked list of who to visit first, a plain-language guess at why, and what it's costing you in rupees.

How it works

1

We measure

Every inverter's actual power output is compared against what it should produce, given the sunlight and temperature that day.

2

We flag

An inverter that keeps underperforming gets flagged, with a plain-language guess at why: dust, a sudden fault, or an outage.

3

You act

See a ranked worklist of who's losing you the most money, so you know exactly which site to visit first.
