Limits of this prototype

  • Only 34 days of data are available in total, so the model has not seen a full year of seasons.
  • Only two plants are included, and each plant has a single on-site weather sensor, so weather (irradiation, temperature) is shared across every inverter at a plant, not measured per inverter.
  • All faults shown anywhere in this app (evaluation metrics, Break It Live) are synthetically injected. There is no ground-truth labeling of real-world faults in this dataset.
